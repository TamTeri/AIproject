- **pricing-001** (ubiquitous): The {EventHub} shall compute the purchase price in one pricing component.
- **pricing-002** (ubiquitous): The {EventHub} shall include base price, quantity, discount eligibility, currency conversion, and total calculation in the pricing component.
- **pricing-003** (event-driven): When a buyer selects a supported currency, the {EventHub} shall display and charge the purchase in that currency using the configured exchange rate.
- **pricing-004** (ubiquitous): The {EventHub} shall use configured exchange rates rather than live exchange-rate data for version one.
- **pricing-005** (event-driven): When an authorized administrator changes an exchange rate through the rate-management screen, the {EventHub} shall persist the updated rate.
- **pricing-006** (event-driven): When the approved student-discount amount, event coverage, eligibility verification, discount-conversion order, and rounding rules are defined, the {EventHub} shall apply those approved pricing policies.
- **pricing-007** (ubiquitous) [Call 4]: The {EventHub} shall compute every price through one function that takes base price, quantity, discount eligibility, and target currency and returns the amount; no page template shall compute money.
- **pricing-008** (ubiquitous) [Call 4]: The {EventHub} shall represent money as integers in the currency's minor unit and exchange rates as exact decimals, as recorded in ADR-005 before the first price was computed.
- **pricing-009** (ubiquitous) [Call 4]: The {EventHub} shall decide student-discount eligibility in one separate, testable function that normalizes the email (trim, ignore case) before checking it.
- **pricing-010** (ubiquitous) [Call 4]: Until the customer confirms the discount percentage, the {EventHub} shall keep it in one named constant marked `[TEMPORARY_MVP_DEFAULT]` and shall refuse to price outside local, development, and test environments.
- **pricing-011** (ubiquitous) [Call 5]: The {EventHub} shall decide student-discount eligibility from the account's current email every time it prices a purchase and again when the buyer pays. If the buyer is no longer eligible when they pay, the {EventHub} shall release the hold, charge nothing, and ask the buyer to start again at the new price (Kenji's finding 1).
- **pricing-012** (optional) [Call 5, `[OPEN]`]: Where the customer requires proof that the buyer owns the USF address (email verification, and re-checking after graduation), the {EventHub} shall grant the discount only to verified addresses. The switch exists (`require_verified_email`) and is off until the customer decides.
- **pricing-013** (event-driven) [Call 6]: When an administrator edits an exchange rate through the admin screen, the {EventHub} shall use the new rate for the next purchase. A change of more than 25% shall need explicit confirmation, and every edit shall be recorded in `exchange_rate_history` (who, old rate, new rate, when).
- **pricing-014** (ubiquitous) [Call 6]: The {EventHub} shall display and charge every amount using the currency's minor unit, with no hardcoded two decimals. A currency whose exponent is missing shall be an error, never a default. Refines pricing-008 and ADR-005 (see ADR-008). Adding a real currency such as JPY to the supported list needs customer sign-off `[OPEN]`; Call 6 only seeds it for testing.

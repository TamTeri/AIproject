- **purchase-001** (event-driven): When a buyer selects a quantity and currency, the {EventHub} shall validate the request on the server before processing it.
- **purchase-002** (unwanted): If a purchase quantity is zero, negative, or otherwise invalid, then the {EventHub} shall reject the purchase request with an invalid-quantity outcome.
- **purchase-003** (unwanted): If a purchase currency is not in the supported currency list, then the {EventHub} shall reject the purchase request with an unsupported-currency outcome.
- **purchase-004** (ubiquitous): The {EventHub} shall process version-one payments as a simulated payment step without an external payment provider.
- **purchase-005** (event-driven): When a buyer completes a successful purchase, the {EventHub} shall create an order containing the event, quantity, currency paid, exchange rate applied, and total.
- **purchase-006** (event-driven): When a purchase succeeds, the {EventHub} shall display an on-screen confirmation.
- **purchase-007** (event-driven): When a buyer submits a purchase attempt, the {EventHub} shall record its timestamp, buyer, event, quantity, currency, and outcome in the audit trail.
- **purchase-008** (unwanted): If a purchase attempt fails because the event is sold out, quantity is invalid, or currency is unsupported, then the {EventHub} shall report the corresponding specific failure outcome.
- **purchase-009** (unwanted): If a purchase exceeds the approved purchase limit, then the {EventHub} shall reject the purchase request.
- **purchase-010** (event-driven): When the approved purchase-limit count and organizational-account exception are defined, the {EventHub} shall enforce those values.
- **purchase-011** (event-driven): When a buyer uses the quick-purchase gesture, the {EventHub} shall apply the same server-side validation and purchase processing as the standard purchase path.
- **purchase-012** (unwanted) [Call 4]: If the simulated payment fails, then the {EventHub} shall release the held tickets exactly once, create no order, and record a failed attempt.
- **purchase-013** (ubiquitous) [Call 4]: The {EventHub} shall charge the price frozen when the checkout hold was created, even if an exchange rate changes before payment.
- **purchase-014** (unwanted) [Call 5]: If the same purchase form is submitted more than once (a double click, a slow page, an impatient thumb or a script retry), then the {EventHub} shall process it only once, create at most one hold or order, show every repeat submission the result of the first one, and record each repeat in the audit trail as replayed. `[TEMPORARY_MVP_DEFAULT]` until the customer confirms the behaviour (requirement gap raised in Call 5).
- **purchase-015** (unwanted) [Call 5]: If one account sends more than 10 purchase requests (checkout starts, payments and quick buys) within 60 seconds, then the {EventHub} shall reject the extra requests until the window has passed, tell the buyer to wait a minute, and record each rejected request as rate-limited. `[TEMPORARY_MVP_DEFAULT]` 10 requests per 60 seconds (Kenji's finding 2).
- **purchase-016** (unwanted) [Call 5]: If a person who is not logged in submits a purchase, then the {EventHub} shall send them to the login page with a message saying why, create no hold or order, and record the attempt in the audit trail as not-authenticated.
- **purchase-017** (unwanted) [Call 5]: If a purchase is rejected, then the {EventHub} shall show a message specific to the reason. A zero quantity, a negative quantity, a quantity that is not a whole number, more tickets than are left (the message says how many are left), a sold-out event, an unsupported currency, the purchase limit and not being logged in each have their own message.
- **purchase-018** (unwanted) [Call 5 test finding]: If an event has already started, then the {EventHub} shall stop selling tickets for it and say that ticket sales have closed. `[TEMPORARY_MVP_DEFAULT]` sales close at the event's start time.
